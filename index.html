Fast checkmate recognition cues
  • Back rank: Look for a rook or queen reaching the home rank while the king is boxed in by its own pawns.
  • Rook + knight: The knight protects the rook and seals escape squares; the Arabian pattern mates the king in a corner.
  • h7/h2 hook: A queen–bishop battery or Greek Gift bishop sacrifice can expose a castled king; calculate the follow-up before sacrificing.
  • Dovetail: A close queen check works when friendly pieces or the edge block the king’s diagonal exits.
  • Bishop + knight clamp: The bishop cuts off a diagonal while the knight delivers checkmate.
  • Smothered mate: A knight finishes when the king is trapped by its own pieces; double check can force the king into the net.
